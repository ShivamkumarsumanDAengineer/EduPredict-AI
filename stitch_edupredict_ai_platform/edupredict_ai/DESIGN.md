## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, forward-looking academic intelligence environment tailored for educators, institutional leaders, and academic advisors. The design dialect balances the rigor of predictive analytics with the approachability required for actionable student interventions.

The visual style is **Corporate Modern with High-Density Precision**:
- High-contrast, crystal-clear information hierarchies optimized for cognitive ease during complex data interpretation.
- Modern SaaS aesthetic featuring crisp container borders, deliberate surface elevations, and deliberate accent pacing.
- The interface evokes feelings of institutional trust, objective clarity, proactive support, and technological sophistication. Data displays reject visual clutter in favor of structured data cards, high-legibility typographic metrics, and context-aware status signaling.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a disciplined academic blue paired with analytical deep navy, punctuated by intelligent purple accents and targeted status feedback:

- **Primary (`#2563EB`)**: Royal Blue anchors primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, interactive controls, and focal data indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Navy commands structural typography, high-priority headings, strong container boundaries, and critical interface anchors.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#8B5CF6`)**: Soft Purple highlights predictive AI features, model confidence scores, automated insights, and forward-looking projections.
- **Semantic Success (`#10B981`)**: Emerald Green denotes positive grade momentum, low-risk student retention statuses, and achieved learning outcomes.
- **Semantic Warning & Critical**: Amber (`#F59E0B`) represents moderate academic risk or declining attendance; Crimson (`#EF4444`) signals high-risk alerts and required immediate intervention.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Background canvas relies on `#F8FAFC`, paired with pure `#FFFFFF` for primary cards and analytical panels. Borders use subtle slate tones (`#E2E8F0` resting, `#CBD5E1` active) to create crisp structural boundaries without visual noise.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headlines and metric values with **Inter** for dense tabular data, operational controls, and body text:

- **Headlines & KPI Displays**: Rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver a modern, approachable, yet confident EdTech presence. Numbers within metric panels leverage tabular lining figures to ensure vertical alignment across dynamic dashboard streams.
- **Body & Tabular Analytics**: Inter provides neutral clarity at small scales, offering high legibility across student rosters, performance percentiles, and multi-column comparison tables.
- **Micro-Labels & Badges**: Set in Inter Medium/SemiBold with slight tracking expansion (`0.02em` - `0.05em`) to maintain crisp readability on colored risk tags and probability markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is a **12-column fluid grid** engineered for data-dense academic monitoring:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Dashboards employ fixed collapsible navigation sidebars (260px) paired with fluid analytical workspaces.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Complex analytics cards reflow from 3 or 4 columns down to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Metrics stack vertically into single-column summary ribbons.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm**: Based on strict multiples of 4px/8px. Component internal padding scales from `space-sm` (badges, inline filters) to `space-lg` (analytics cards, risk assessment containers).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is communicated through **Crisp Borders and Ambient Micro-Shadows**:

- **Surface Structure**: Surfaces sit on a soft slate canvas (`#F8FAFC`). Primary cards use crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Resting Elevation**: Subdued ambient shadow (`0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`) ensuring cards feel grounded without floating erratically.
- **Hover & Active Elevation**: Interactive modules transition cleanly to a focused elevation (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`) with border accentuation (`#CBD5E1`).
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts**: Deep modal overlays use a slight navy tint (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`) accompanied by elevated panels with `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard:

- Standard controls, inputs, dropdowns, and buttons feature `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii.
- Analytical data cards, modal containers, and student profile sections utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) to maintain a refined, approachable SaaS appearance.
- Segmented status pills, AI prediction tags, and filter chips use a full pill radius (`9999px`) to create clear differentiation between data containers and interactive markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) background, white text, 8px radius, height 40px, padding `0 1rem`. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: subtle inset shadow.
- **Secondary**: Deep Navy (`#0F172A`) outline or filled background for institutional governance actions.
- **AI Action Variant**: Gradient border or solid Soft Purple tint (`#F5F3FF` background with `#8B5CF6` text and border) for predictive runs and synthetic intervention generation.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Neutral slate text with `#F1F5F9` background on hover.

### Analytics & Data Cards
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, 16px corner radius, and `1.5rem` inner padding.
- Equipped with a top micro-header housing the metric title, context tooltip, and a status pill indicator.
- Metric values use `metric-display` in Deep Navy (`#0F172A`), paired with a trend indicator (arrow icon + percentage in `#10B981` or `#EF4444`).

### Badge Metrics & Prediction Chips
- **Low Risk / On Track**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`.
- **Moderate Risk**: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`.
- **Critical Intervention**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`.
- **AI Probability Score**: Background `#F5F3FF`, text `#6D28D9`, border 1px solid `#DDD6FE`, prefixed with a subtle sparkle icon.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- Clean white surface, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, 8px radius, height 40px, font Inter `0.875rem`.
- Focus state applies a 2px Royal Blue outline offset by 1px with `#2563EB` border.
- Integrated search inputs include clear prefix icons in `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18px x 18px with 4px radius (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Default border `#94A3B8`. Checked state fills with `#2563EB` bearing a crisp white checkmark or center dot.

### Data Tables & Roster Lists
- Alternating row hover states with `#F8FAFC`. Header row uses `#F1F5F9` with sticky positioning, uppercase `label-sm` in `#64748B`.
- Row cells feature vertical dividers eliminated in favor of clean 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`. Inline mini-progress bars visualize attendance rate and GPA forecasts directly within row cells.
